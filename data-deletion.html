User Data Deletion

Forte SMAS · Last updated October 28, 2026

You can remove Forte SMAS's access and have all of your data deleted at any time.

1. Remove the app's access

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Forte SMAS and select Remove.

This immediately invalidates the access tokens the app holds.

2. Request deletion of stored data

Contact tacanaliyeva@gmail.com and ask for your data to be deleted. Include your Facebook name or the Page name you connected. All stored data (Page and Instagram account IDs, access tokens, statistics and post history) is deleted within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation.
