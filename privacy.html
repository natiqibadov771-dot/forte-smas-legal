Privacy Policy

Forte SMAS · Last updated October 28, 2026

Forte SMAS is a private social media management tool. It is used by its operator to prepare and publish posts to Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts that the operator manages. It is not offered to the general public.

Information we collect

  • When you connect an account with Facebook Login for Business: your Facebook user ID, the names and IDs of the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts you choose to grant, and the access tokens Meta issues for them.
  • Page and Instagram account statistics returned by the Meta Graph API, such as follower counts, reach and post views.
  • Your Telegram user ID and chat ID, used only to send you previews and notifications from the bot.
  • The content of posts prepared in the tool (text, images, videos).

How we use it

  • To publish posts to the Pages and Instagram accounts you connected, only after you approve them.
  • To show statistics about those accounts to you.
  • To generate post text, article titles and descriptions are sent to the OpenAI API. No Facebook or Instagram user data is sent to OpenAI.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private PostgreSQL database controlled by the operator. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is connected. You can disconnect at any time and request deletion of all your data as described on the data deletion page. Deletion is completed within 30 days.
